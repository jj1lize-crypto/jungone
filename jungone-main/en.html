A card-and-online garden for family care conversations before crisis.

  3. Listen Choose how you want to be heard.
  4. Keep Leave a trace, or let it go.
What is this?

Garden is a tool where families ask, listen, pause, and return — tending their conversations like a small garden.

Care can be attention, but sometimes intrusion. Rather than fix or convince each other, Garden helps you gently adjust the distance and the level of help you need. Not speaking is also an answer. Not leaving a trace is also a choice.

Listening cards are not for one side to conform to the other — they are a way for both to tune the pace and shape of the conversation together.

To preserve the color and scent of each other's lives, Garden practices caring from a healthy distance.
